# Policy: price-sensitivity-risk
For customers flagged at elevated risk of price-sensitive churn: offer a
loyalty discount on the current plan tier, or a contract-length incentive
(month-to-month customers converting to a one-year contract get a reduced
introductory rate). Do not offer discounts exceeding company guideline caps
without manager approval.

# Policy: dissatisfaction-risk
For customers flagged at elevated risk of dissatisfaction-driven churn:
escalate to a retention specialist for a service-quality outreach call
within 5 business days, rather than a generic discount offer. If the
customer lacks tech support and has fiber internet, proactively offer a
complimentary tech-support trial period.

# Policy: competitive-loss-risk
For customers flagged at elevated risk of competitive loss: retention team
may match a documented competitor offer within approved limits, focusing on
month-to-month and electronic-check-payment customers, who show the
strongest association with this risk category.

# Policy: non-behavioral-risk
For customers flagged at elevated risk of non-behavioral churn (e.g. likely
move): no retention offer is appropriate -- this risk is largely outside
the company's control. Focus retention resources on the other three
categories instead.
